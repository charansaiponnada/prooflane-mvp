## Description (copy everything between the lines)

---

AI agents are starting to move real money. When one of those payments is disputed, who can prove what actually happened?

ProofLane is the evidence gateway for AI agents. Every consequential action — like releasing a payment — is checked against policy and sealed into a cryptographic receipt *before* it runs. Auditors, customers and regulators can verify that receipt themselves, without trusting the company's logs or seeing its sensitive data.

The evidence does not have to come from a system you trust.

🎬 In this video
✅ The problem: today's only evidence is the operator's own logs
✅ A $48,200 agent payment is policy-checked and sealed before money moves
✅ An auditor verifies the receipt in their own browser
✅ Only one field (the approval ID) is disclosed — everything else stays hidden
❌ An insider changes $48,200 → $4,820 — verification fails instantly
✅ The evidence pack exports and verifies anywhere
